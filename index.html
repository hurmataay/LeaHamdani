The problem: Centene's internal Jobs Hub platform had no onboarding material, and support teams were fielding the same questions on repeat.

The approach: as the first hire into a newly created role, Lea analyzed recurring support tickets to find where employees actually got stuck, then scoped, scripted, and revised a full tutorial video from a rough draft to a polished, distributed script. "Create an instructional YouTube tutorial video for Centene's internal Jobs Hub platform to guide employees on effectively using the platform for job searching and applications... By documenting the workflow, rationale, and lessons learned, the organization ensures continuous improvement in future projects." — from the project playbook, written by Lea Hamdani, August 2023

Objective: Create an instructional YouTube tutorial video for Centene's internal Jobs Hub platform to guide employees on effectively using the platform for job searching and applications.

I. Planning phase. Establish the goal of the tutorial video: to educate employees on utilizing Jobs Hub efficiently. Determine the key topics to cover, such as accessing Jobs Hub, using tabs, and completing the Talent Profile. Outline the video's structure, ensuring a logical flow and clear presentation of information, organized into sections: Introduction, Main Overview Page, Tabs in Jobs Hub, Completing Talent Profile, and Conclusion.

II. Execution phase. Write a professional script based on the project's rough draft, ensuring clarity and adherence to objectives, then review and refine it for accuracy and conciseness. Distribute the outline among team members for further revisions, fostering an unbiased approach and encouraging the expression of diverse opinions. Learn Camtasia and review its tutorials before recording, then record the video in a clear and engaging manner using suitable visual aids and graphics, and edit it to maintain a professional appearance so the content flows seamlessly.

III. Evaluation phase. Conduct an internal review of the video with team members for any further revisions, and gather feedback on content, presentation, and overall effectiveness.

IV. Lessons learned. Identify lessons learned throughout the project's lifecycle, such as effective communication strategies, successful content approaches, and areas for improvement. Summarize the critical lessons learned from the project, highlighting best practices and areas of improvement.

Conclusion. The creation of the Jobs Hub Tutorial Video provides a valuable resource for employees, empowering them with the knowledge and tools to effectively use Jobs Hub. By documenting the workflow, rationale, and lessons learned, the organization ensures continuous improvement in future projects and fosters a culture of knowledge sharing and collaboration.

ResultPresented directly to Centene's Head of HR and adopted company wide, reducing how often employees needed to go through support for basic platform questions.

The problem: a campus kindness recognition program had a strong mission but almost no nominations, run mostly by volunteer faculty with limited time for promotion.

The approach: Lea led a 17 question primary research survey across a seven person student agency, which surfaced the real issue: 77% of respondents had never heard of the program, and for many, the survey itself was their first exposure to it. Gaming commentary

Are we moving beyond Fast 8 in Patch 16.5?

A breakdown of a major Teamfight Tactics balance shift, written for her own following, arguing that the patch wasn't a quick fix but a deliberate move away from one dominant strategy toward a more varied, mid game focused meta.

Personal gaming writing, February 2026
Read the full piece +

The "Fast 8" playstyle method was dominant because players saw a clear tension point in the extra economy and time needed to reach level 9. Around round 4.1 to 4.2, players would rush level 8 and exhaust their economy trying to reach a three star four cost as soon as possible. By late game, if you didn't have that upgraded four cost, your chances of hitting first place were very unlikely. A top four finish was still possible depending on the lobby, but it felt like most winning comps were best promised with that three star four cost carry.

Patch 16.5 and the shift toward reroll compositions. Patch 16.5 is trying to broaden the current meta by shifting power away from fast 8 comps and toward reroll comps surrounding three star two and three cost units. The most impactful change to me was the buffing of two and three cost reroll comps. It feels like Riot is shifting more power back to mid cost champions rather than forcing players into a long wait to level 8. The game pace is now more mid game focused instead of late game centered. Rerolls have always felt like that quick ladder climb strategy. One tricks will 100% benefit the most from this shift, reroll comps tend to be more limiting compared to flexible or vertical comps, but they reward commitment. It feels more rewarding for force comp users.

Riot's data driven balance philosophy. Riot operates from a data driven correction standpoint. Teamfight Tactics, which is an auto chess genre game, isn't as mechanically complex to shift compared to other popular genres like MOBAs, MMORPGs, or FPS titles, but it does require constantly adapting to patch notes. The development team has shown that they can be reactive, the recent Aurelion Sol nerf in Patch 16.3 was clearly a response to players abusing it to climb ranks. Patch 16.5 feels different. It feels directional. It doesn't come across as a fix and error patch like the Aurelion Sol situation. Instead, it feels intentional.

Why constant patches matter for live service games. Frequent patching lets players know the game is in constant development, that new content is meant to be played, and that the company actually cares about its audience and user feedback. Without meaningful balance updates, Teamfight Tactics would most likely become a stale and repetitive board game. Constant meta changes excite people, even if they can also be stressful when certain strategies become abusable in order to win.

Players should understand that the development team does listen. The Senior Game Design Director and Lead Designer of Teamfight Tactics, known as "Mortdog," regularly answers player questions and explains design decisions in his podcast Walk the Dog. Knowing the "what" and the "why" behind patches makes you a smarter player because you're able to adapt and apply that understanding to your own playstyle.

If the fast 8 comp is no longer the only path to first, what does your playstyle look like now?

Opinion

Epic layoffs reveal an industry empathy crisis

An op-ed written after Epic Games' 2026 layoffs, connecting her own goal of breaking into gaming to a broader argument that poor budgeting and short term thinking cost studios the very talent that built their success.

Individual writing portfolio, 2026
Read the full piece +

When Epic Games announced layoffs affecting hundreds of employees, the response followed a now familiar script. Apologies were issued. Leadership acknowledged the "difficult decisions." Promises were made about long term sustainability.

And then the industry moved on.

But for many of us who are trying to enter this industry, it didn't sit right.

I care about this topic because I want to work in gaming. A lot of my interests lie in companies like Riot Games, and just weeks ago, layoffs hit their publishing division as well. I have mutuals who work in the industry, and when Epic's layoffs were announced, gaming Twitter turned into a stream of people sending condolences to employees who suddenly lost their jobs.

That alone says enough.

This wasn't just another corporate restructuring story. Reports revealed that among those affected was an employee with terminal cancer who would lose access to life saving insurance. That is not just a business decision. That is a human failure.

I've been playing video games since I was three years old. I grew up on console and now play on PC. Gaming used to be escapism. Now, with how connected everything is, it's a full community. Even though I haven't played Fortnite since 2018, I still care about the people behind these games. And this layoff was no joke at all.

What makes this situation worse is how it happened. It's Q1. Why are we laying off people now, and at such a large scale? Reports suggest cuts impacted a massive portion of teams. That raises real questions about planning and priorities.

Fortnite has had countless high profile collaborations with celebrities and brands, many of which were likely incredibly expensive. At some point, it starts to feel like over budgeting caught up with them. You can't pour money into massive collaborations and then turn around and say cutting employees is the only option.

And then come the apologies. Tim Sweeney addressed the layoffs publicly, but at this point, it feels like an extremely late excuse. It comes across like someone backed into a corner, dealing with consequences much later than expected, like a hurricane no one realized was building until it hit.

Supporters will argue that layoffs are part of doing business. And to a certain extent, they are. I study PR and marketing. I understand that companies need to make financial decisions to survive.

But cutting such a large percentage of your workforce is not a small adjustment. It reflects deeper issues, especially around budgeting and long term planning.

As a broke college student trying to enter this field, this is honestly terrifying. If people with years of experience and established careers can be let go like this, what does that mean for those of us trying to get our foot in the door?

These layoffs don't just impact current employees. They push future talent away.

The gaming industry markets itself as innovative, community driven, and people focused. But moments like this reveal a different reality. The people who build these worlds are often treated as expendable when financial decisions catch up.

At the end of the day, this is a lesson not just for Epic Games, but for the entire industry. Poor budgeting and short term decision making can lead to losing the very teams that made success possible in the first place.

And if that continues, the industry risks losing more than just employees. It risks losing the trust of the people who want to be part of it.

Ed Sheeran to Headline Taylor Swift's Wedding
Longtime friend to perform at the party of the century

Watch Hill, RI, Feb. 16, Taylor Swift today announced on Instagram Live that Grammy Award winning singer songwriter Ed Sheeran will be the lead music act during her June 13, 2026 wedding celebration to Kansas City Chiefs' Travis Kelce.

Swift's latest 2026 album continued to reinforce her in the music industry, extending a career that's been successful for nearly two decades. As Swift strives for professional endurance, Kelce takes an opposite stance and has publicly discussed closing a chapter of his career, signaling a transition into the next phase of his life, whether it's on the field or not.

Swift and Kelce first drew attention to the public eye back in 2023, quickly becoming one of the most popular celebrity couples, combining both sports and entertainment fans. Earlier this year, their engagement was confirmed, transitioning more into a permanent partnership. The June wedding ceremony represents a new beginning following years defined by stadium tours and championship seasons.

Sheeran's role in the wedding displays a long lasting friendship that has evolved alongside Swift's different stages of both career and now life. The two artists collaborated on the hit 2012 pop song "Everything Has Changed," displaying that even a single connection can alter perspective and mark the start of something new. More than a decade later, the themes of growth and transformation remain parallel to Swift's life as she enters a different chapter of her life.

"Ed has been there for some of the most important moments in my career and in my personal life," Swift said. "It feels meaningful to have someone who has seen so many seasons with me help lead the music on this day."

Sheeran, who is currently on the "LOOP Tour" for his newest album, Play, said he rearranged his schedule to participate in his friend's ceremony. Though known for maintaining a relatively private lifestyle and known for being off the digital grid, Sheeran said his friendship with Swift has remained constant despite distance and demanding schedules.

"Taylor and I have shared music and long conversations for years," Sheeran said. "Being part of this moment is about showing up for a friend who has shown up for me."

In recent interviews, Sheeran has emphasized the importance of intentional connection, even while navigating through worldwide tours. Swift has similarly credited longtime collaborators and trusted friendships as anchors throughout her career.

The wedding celebration is expected to be held in a private venue attended strictly by close friends and family in Watch Hill, Rhode Island. Additional details regarding the ceremony have not been disclosed at the moment. The announcement will be distributed nationally and internationally through PR Newswire.
